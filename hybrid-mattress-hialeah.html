Why a Hybrid

Spring support, with a deeper comfort layer.

A hybrid mattress pairs a spring support core with a thicker comfort layer of foam or latex. The springs keep the mattress breathable and responsive, while the comfort layer relieves pressure at the shoulders and hips. It is a good middle ground if a traditional spring mattress feels too bouncy and an all-foam mattress feels too firm.

Buying a hybrid from the factory that makes it means no middleman. Our concept has been the same for more than fifty years: make the best possible mattress at the best price in the market.

A hybrid mattress combines a spring (coil) support core with thicker comfort layers of foam or latex on top. You get the support and airflow of springs with the pressure relief of foam or latex.
