Custom sizes

How long does a custom mattress take?

It depends on the size and the materials you choose. We give you a date when we quote, and if you are in a hurry, tell us: every custom mattress is built here, so we can tell you right away what is possible.

How do I measure for a custom mattress?

Measure the inside of the bed frame: width, length and the height you want, in inches. If a corner is rounded or cut, or the shape is uneven, trace it on paper or cardboard and bring it in, or text us a photo with the measurements.

How much does a custom size mattress cost?

It depends on the size and materials. The quote is free: call or text (786) 306-7842 with your measurements.
