Made in our own factory

Every mattress is built in Hialeah. We keep the materials here and build to order, so you pick the feel instead of settling for whatever is on a showroom floor.

No middleman

When chain stores buy mattresses, they push for the lowest cost to maximize profit, and that usually forces manufacturers to cut quality. We sell directly from our factory to you.

Any size, any shape

Antique beds, odd sizes, RVs, campers and boats. If a store does not carry your size, we build it.
