Boat and yacht mattresses

V-berths, cabins, angled corners and tight layouts. We make the mattress to the shape of your cabin, with marine-grade materials chosen to handle humidity.

RV, camper and motorhome mattresses

Short queens, cut corners and thin platforms are normal in RVs. We build to the size and height your bed actually needs, in the comfort you want on the road.

Bring your template

  1. Trace the shape

    Lay paper or cardboard on the berth and trace the edge.

  2. Write the numbers

    Mark the longest and widest points, and the height you can fit.

  3. Send it or bring it

    Text a photo or bring the template to 2655 W 76th St, Hialeah.

  4. Get your quote

    We confirm the materials, the price and the date.

Boat and RV mattress questions

Do you make mattresses for any boat shape?

Yes. V-berths, angled corners, curved edges and cabin layouts that no standard mattress fits. Bring a template and we cut to it.

Can you make a mattress for my RV or camper?

Yes. We make RV, camper and motorhome mattresses to the measurements of your bed, including short sizes and cut corners.

How long does it take?

It depends on the shape and the materials. We give you a date when we quote, so call or text (786) 306-7842 with your measurements or a photo of your template.
